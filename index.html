About

Founded by IT professionals with over 50 years of combined experience in management and technical roles across Education, Insurance, Finance, and Data Center Applications, our team has worked with global leaders like HP, IBM, Tech Mahindra, Wipro, and BMC. Our flagship platform, CampusTrack, offers a user-friendly solution for all educational stakeholders, addressing diverse needs with simplicity and power. Bitloka has since evolved beyond EdTech, integrating LMS, Cloud services, Observability, and Generative AI features.
