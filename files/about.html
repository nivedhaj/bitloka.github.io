About Us




Founded by seasoned IT professionals with over 50 years of combined experience in both management and technical roles across diverse domains such as Education, Insurance, Finance, and Data Center Applications. Our team members have honed their expertise at esteemed multinational corporations including HP, IBM, Tech Mahindra, Wipro, and BMC.                       Our innovative flagship solution, CampusTrack, is a sophisticated yet user-friendly platform designed for all stakeholders within an educational institution. From individual contributors to independent learners, CampusTrack addresses diverse needs with simplicity and power. Over time, CampusTrack has expanded beyond traditional EdTech, incorporating features related to Learning Management Systems (LMS), Cloud services, Observability, and Generative Artificial Intelligence (GenAI).
