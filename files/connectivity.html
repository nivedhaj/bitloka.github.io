Connectivity
  In educational institutions, effective communication is essential for engagement and collaboration. Utilizing features like SMS, Email, Notifications, Chat messages, and Chatbots ensures seamless connectivity among stakeholders.
 
 

SMS
  • The SMS feature enables quick and direct communication with       stakeholders, including teachers, students, and parents.
  • Important announcements, event notifications, and emergency alerts       can be instantly communicated via SMS, ensuring timely information        dissemination.
  • Automated SMS notifications can be sent for attendance updates,                 exam schedules, and other critical academic information.

     
     
    Email
  • Emails serve as a formal channel for communication, allowing                            educational institutions to send detailed messages, newsletters, and               official announcements.
  • Documentation: Important documents, such as report cards, circulars,        and official notices, can be shared securely through email
  • Emails facilitate two-way communication, allowing stakeholders to              respond, ask questions, and engage in a more detailed exchange of              information.


    Notification
      The notification feature in a web-based and mobile application is a critical component that ensures timely communication, engagement, and information dissemination within the educational institution. Users receive notifications directly on the web-based CampusTrack platform when they log in, ensuring they don't miss important updates. Mobile applications leverage push notifications to reach users instantly, even when the application is not actively in use.
  • Integrated with the calendar module, academics module, LMS        module,etc to send reminders about upcoming events, meetings,        attendance, examinations, and other important dates, etc
  • Notifications provide instant alerts to users about important updates,        announcements, events, and deadlines.


    Chat messages
    Integrated chat message feature greatly enhances the communication and collaboration among stakeholders.
  • The chat messaging feature enables real-time communication between students, teachers, parents, and administrators, fostering instant collaboration.
  • Teachers and students can use chat for project discussions, clarification of doubts, and collaborative learning, promoting a more interactive educational experience.
  • Facilitates direct communication between parents and teachers, enabling discussions about student progress, upcoming events, and other relevant information.


    • Chatbot



    Chatbot feature enhance user experience, and provide instant support.

  • It offers round-the-clock assistance, providing instant responses to user queries, thereby ensuring timely support for students, parents, and staff.
  • It can guide new users through the onboarding process, helping them navigate CampusTrack platform and understand its features.
  • It is capable of answering frequently asked questions, retrieving information on schedules, exam dates, academic resources, and other relevant data.
  • It sends automated reminders for events, deadlines, and important dates, ensuring that users stay informed about upcoming activities.
